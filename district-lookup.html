Covers San Antonio, Austin, El Paso, Waco, Del Rio, Midland, and Pecos. Most zip codes starting with 780xx-789xx (San Antonio/Austin area), 790xx-799xx (El Paso/Lubbock), and 76xxx (Waco area) fall here.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which bankruptcy district covers San Antonio?

San Antonio is in the Western District of Texas. The courthouse is located at 615 E Houston St, San Antonio, TX 78205. Most San Antonio metro zip codes (780xx-789xx) fall within this district.

How do I know which Texas bankruptcy district I'm in?

Texas has four federal bankruptcy districts: Western (San Antonio/Austin/El Paso), Southern (Houston/Corpus Christi), Northern (Dallas/Fort Worth), and Eastern (Tyler/Beaumont). Your zip code determines your district.

Does the Western District of Texas also cover Austin?

Yes. The Western District of Texas covers a large area including San Antonio, Austin, Waco, El Paso, Del Rio, Midland, and Pecos. The Austin courthouse is at 903 San Jacinto Blvd, Austin, TX 78701.
